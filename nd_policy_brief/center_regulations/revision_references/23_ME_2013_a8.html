SUMMARY STATEMENT

This rule is established to govern the licensing of Child Care Facilities in the State of Maine, including Child Care Centers, Nursery Schools, Small Child Care Facilities, and Other Programs, and to protect the health, safety, and welfare of the Children served.

AUTHORITY

This rule is promulgated under the authority of 22 M.R.S. Chapters 1661, 1663, 1673 and 1675.

EFFECTIVE DATE

September 27, 2021
